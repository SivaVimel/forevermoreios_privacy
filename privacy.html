Overview

Forevermore: Wedding Countdown does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Information you enter

Everything you add is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widgets:

  • Your countdown title, wedding date and time
  • Your style choices and settings
  • A photo, if you choose one as your background

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

If you pick a photo as your background, the app receives only that photo and keeps a copy in its own storage on your device. It never browses your photo library.

If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add the wallpaper to your photo library. The app can only add images; it can't read your existing photos.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone; there is no notification server. The Lock Screen countdown is also created on your device. You can turn either off at any time in the app or in the iPhone Settings app.

Shortcuts

The app offers optional actions for the Shortcuts app so you can change your wallpaper on the day. They run on your iPhone and only read your countdown and chosen wallpaper.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone, including widgets and any photo you chose.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
